About me

I came to Software Engineering through mathematics. I'm drawn to the structure underneath complex systems: how data moves between them, how the parts integrate, and how small design decisions show up in production. At York University, that curiosity has grown into a focus on software, data and AI.

My co-op experience as a Junior Developer with the Ontario Public Service was a turning point for me. I started with Oracle Analytics Publisher, but my curiosity quickly took me beyond my original role into Siebel CRM configuration, OpenSearch log analysis, and cross-system integrations. I went from learning how large enterprise systems work to taking ownership of production-ready tasks and seeing my work become part of real workflows. That experience showed me how much I enjoy learning by building and solving problems that have a real impact.

I've also explored machine learning, data, and IoT through research and personal projects. I've trained ML models on high-dimensional datasets for neurological disease research, worked with C++ sensor networks, and built projects

I'm looking for software, data and machine learning roles where I can ship useful work and keep learning from a strong team.
